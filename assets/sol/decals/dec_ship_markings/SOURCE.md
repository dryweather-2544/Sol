# dec_ship_markings

Procedural vector art + IBM Plex (OFL, `fonts/ibm_plex/`, unmodified). 18 decals, one **albedo-only** file each (paint on paint
has no relief; the wall's own ORM shows through). **Integration table: `decals.csv`** (generated by the baker: size in metres,
depth, `modulate`, `signal_hex`, placement, `tiles_x`). Files: `dec_ship_markings_<name>_albedo.png` (RGBA8 sRGB).

- **Bake:** `godot --path lookdev -s res://bake/bake_ship_markings.gd` (needs a window: text is drawn through
  `bake/canvas_capture.gd`). Deterministic. **Validate:** `godot --headless --path lookdev -s res://validate/check_decal.gd -- dec_ship_markings`
- **Scale rule:** size_m = px / 512 for everything except `pad_mark` (1024 px over 10 m, ~102 px/m, seen from the air). This
  differs by a few percent from the brief's round sizes (e.g. arrows 0.3125 m, ids 0.203 x 0.055 m) so text is not stretched.
  Room names: cap height 100 mm (cap-height ratio 0.698 of the em for Plex), widths fitted to the text and rounded to x4 px.
- **Colour:** text, arrows, ids, room names, FIRE EXT and pad mark are authored near-white (225) and coloured with
  `Decal.modulate` (csv `modulate`: composite_dark `#2A2D30` for names/ids on paint walls, `#B9B6AD` on primer walls and for the
  pad, `#E8A33A` for arrows, `#D9442E` for FIRE EXT). PRESS/PULL (signal_amber) and the hazard stripe (amber / `#2A2D30`, 100 mm
  colour pairs at 45 deg, 10 periods across 512 px so it **tiles along x**) are authored in final colours (modulate FFFFFF).
- **Wear:** noise removes alpha at lettering edges: 1.6% of lettering area lost (limit 15%) - deliberately light.
- **Validation (2026-10-18):** `check_decal` PASS for all 18 (RGBA; size = csv; border clear; luminance 30-240; PRESS/PULL mean
  colour = #E8A33A exactly; hazard stripe wrap-seam ratio within limit). Self-tests prove it rejects an opaque border and a
  non-tiling stripe.
- **Review shots:** `lookdev/shots/dec_ship_markings_{sheet,game_light,proposed_light,pad}.png` (the sheet is ~345 px/m, about
  two thirds of 1:1, because the preview window stays 1152 px wide).
- **Concerns (claude only, stills):** PRESS/PULL (64 x 20 px) and the ids (104 x 28 px) are tiny; they are readable only within
  about 1 m. If they must be read from further, double the px, not the size. Wear is subtle. The room shots show only part of the set.
