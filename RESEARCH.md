# Rodina research archive

This branch (`research/rodina-dossier`) publishes the project's textual Rodina research and Sol
implementation findings for other humans and LLM agents.

## Start here

- [research/README.md](research/README.md) — map of the archive and reading order.
- [research/LLM_GUIDE.md](research/LLM_GUIDE.md) — concise instructions/evidence rules for agents.
- [research/PROVENANCE.md](research/PROVENANCE.md) — what is original, what is third-party, and
  what is deliberately not redistributed.
- [docs/RODINA_TECHNICAL_RESEARCH.md](docs/RODINA_TECHNICAL_RESEARCH.md) — flight, camera, entry,
  landing, planets and AI evidence.
- [docs/RODINA_VISUAL_RESEARCH.md](docs/RODINA_VISUAL_RESEARCH.md) — visual identity research.
- [docs/RODINA_VISUAL_P1_RESULTS.md](docs/RODINA_VISUAL_P1_RESULTS.md) and
  [docs/RODINA_VISUAL_P23_RESULTS.md](docs/RODINA_VISUAL_P23_RESULTS.md) — implemented visual
  profile results, tests and limitations.
- [research/rodina-starfall-reference/](research/rodina-starfall-reference/) — the existing wider
  systems research archive.
- [research/rodina-visual-observations/](research/rodina-visual-observations/) — source URLs,
  timestamps, observations and palette measurements.

No extracted Rodina code, shaders, models, textures, videos or reference screenshots are included
in this branch; see `research/PROVENANCE.md`.

This is a research/publication branch. It is not merged into `main` and was not pushed to `main`.
